Marvalous Competitions – Account Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of your Marvalous Competitions account and associated personal data, please contact our support team.

How to request account deletion

Send an email from the email address associated with your Marvalous Competitions account and request that your account is deleted.

Request account deletion

What happens next?

We will verify your request and delete your account and associated personal data where applicable.

Some information may be retained where required for legal, regulatory, fraud prevention, accounting or record-keeping purposes.

Marvalous Competitions is available to UK residents aged 18 and over.
